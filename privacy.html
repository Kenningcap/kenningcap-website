Privacy policy: WhatsApp line

This WhatsApp line is for the firm's internal use by its own principals, team and partners. It is not a public or customer service line, and the firm does not use it to contact the public.

Who we are

Kenning Capital is a trading name of MEHUTAHUTA TRADING AND INVESTMENTS (PTY) LTD (registration 2013/005096/07). Contact: nic@kenningcap.com.

What this policy covers

Messages sent to the firm's WhatsApp Business line.

What is collected

  • The sender's WhatsApp phone number, or the WhatsApp username identifier Meta provides instead of a number.
  • The message content: text, voice notes, images and documents.
  • The time each message was received.

Why

To receive and answer messages from the firm's own principals, team and partners. Messages from anyone else are not acted on.

Where it goes

  • Meta, as WhatsApp's operator, processes messages and stores them at rest in South Africa. Meta may process them outside South Africa for up to 60 minutes.
  • The firm's own Google Cloud project in Johannesburg holds each message briefly until it is delivered, then deletes it from that store.
  • Voice notes are transcribed by software running in that Google Cloud project.
  • Each accepted message is delivered as an email to the firm's assistant mailbox in the firm's Google Workspace email. Replies sent back through the line pass through the line's own mailbox there.
  • The firm's AI assistant reads delivered messages in order to answer them.

How long it is kept

  • Messages are deleted from the Google Cloud store after delivery.
  • Messages delivered to the firm's mailboxes are kept as the firm's email.
  • Records that link a reply to a chat expire after 24 hours and are removed within 2 days.
  • Short-lived delivery markers are deleted automatically.
  • Messages that cannot be delivered are kept for review until deleted by staff.
  • Meta's own retention applies to Meta's copies.

Your rights

You can ask what is held about you, or ask for it to be corrected, by writing to nic@kenningcap.com.

Deleting your data

To ask for your WhatsApp data to be deleted, write to nic@kenningcap.com, saying which WhatsApp number you messaged from.

The firm then deletes:

  • its copies of your WhatsApp messages;
  • any stored link between your number and your WhatsApp username identifier.

Meta's own copies follow Meta's retention.
